## Paragraph re: data

This dataset includes information about the NYC transit station’s
entrances and exits across locations and routes. In additon to
informaiton about the division, line, station names, station locations,
and routes, it provides information about entrance types, entry, exit,
vending, staffing and staff hours, ada compliance, if there is free
crossover, cross streets, and entrance locations, with a total of 32
variables. The dataset was reduced to include 19 variables: line,
station name, station latitude, station longitude, routes 1-11, vending,
entrance type, entry, and ada compliance.The entry variable was
converted from a character yes/no variable to a logical variable. There
are 19 columns and 1868 rows
